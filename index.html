Why I love Football

Football is a sport I love because it keeps you guessing and on your toes. No two results are ever the same, and the players keep getting better; the dribbles are exciting, the shots are exquisite, and it just keeps me coming back for more. The fans are always excited, whether it's an away or home fixture, and the atmosphere is always jumping. It is the best sport in the world, with the most fans following it; you only have to look at the last World Cup, which happened this year.
